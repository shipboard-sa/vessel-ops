<details><summary>#2 Windlass - Clutch Check & Adjust</summary>

- **caution - old:** Drop and re-set on the clutch a few times to feel for smooth progressive grip. If it slips loaded or will not free-fall, strip and clean the cones - do not just tighten the clutch nut, that masks worn cones and shears keys. Dual-chainwheel note (Maxwell 2200 HWC manual, "2200 HWC Dble Chn Sgle Drm"): both chainwheels AND the center warping drum share one common shaft - each is coupled to it by its own independent clutch. Before powering one chainwheel (or the drum), back the OTHER chainwheel's clutch nut off until it stops - if its clutch is left tightened, it is still coupled to the shaft and will get driven too, paying out or hauling in that anchor/rode uncontrolled. The manual states this explicitly for the drum ("insert the lever into EACH of the clutch nuts and back EACH off") but only describes a single clutch in its generic anchor up/down steps - confirm the same applies port/starboard whichever chainwheel is idle before use.

Before powering one chainwheel (or the drum), back the OTHER chainwheel's clutch nut off - a clutch left tight is still coupled and will pay out or haul in that rode uncontrolled.
